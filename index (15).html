My growth approach

How I'd approach the first 100 paying customers.

A proposed, test-and-learn approach for a WhatsApp CRM and automation product like BetaXLab. The goal is to identify a focused customer segment, prove value, and build a repeatable acquisition motion.

100 customers

A milestone to work toward through focused segments, customer conversations, measurable experiments, and consistent follow-up — not a claim of past results.

01

Choose a focused starting segment

Prioritize lead-driven businesses such as real estate teams, clinics, education providers, and agencies. Identify where missed WhatsApp enquiries and inconsistent follow-ups create visible pain.

02

Build a qualified prospect list

Research businesses, decision-makers, agencies, and relevant Shopify or e-commerce communities. Organize prospects by segment, use case, and likely need.

03

Start conversations and demonstrate value

Use personalized outreach, short product walkthroughs, and practical examples to show how a shared inbox, lead CRM, and automated follow-ups can help teams respond consistently.

04

Convert trials and learn from objections

Support onboarding, follow up with trial users, understand blockers, and capture feedback on setup, workflows, and pricing. Use those insights to improve conversion.

05

Turn early wins into repeatable growth

Document customer stories, referral opportunities, partner channels, and the outreach messages that work. Track lead-to-demo, demo-to-trial, and trial-to-paid conversion.
